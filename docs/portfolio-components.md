# Selected research and verification capabilities

The engine currently offers bounded exact-sequence experiments and selected CSV/JSON preflight utilities. It does not advertise every component of the broader research portfolio as integrated.

## Exact sequence experiments

The workflow uses integer and rational inputs, bounded polynomial and recurrence models, held-out checks and replayable results. A finite fit is evidence within the tested bounds; it is not proof of a unique generator, scientific law or novelty.

## Research integration

Further mathematical and semantic components should enter through explicit adapters with source provenance, license review, current behavior tests and clear output claims. Source-grounded arithmetic, structured claim checks and consistency analysis are potential delivery applications; their actual supported scope must be demonstrated before use.

Preserve distinct implementations and their tests when reconciling sources. Generated artifacts should be regenerated from canonical sources. Keep unsupported components recoverable and label limits accurately.

## Attribution

The bundled CSV/JSON tools retain their MIT notice and a provenance manifest. Additional components must preserve their own file-level licenses and required attribution. Code, prose, third-party datasets and retrieved reference material can have different terms; an engine license does not replace them.

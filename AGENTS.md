# Contributor requirements

Protect private credentials and customer, business, and personal data. Keep sensitive material out of public source, documentation, logs, artifacts, and commits.

Preserve required licenses, attribution, and legitimate authorship when reusing or modifying material.

Ground technical and public-facing claims in current source and reproducible evidence. State limitations accurately and distinguish verified results from assumptions.

Run tests appropriate to the changes, update relevant documentation, and review the diff for unintended changes and private information before committing.
